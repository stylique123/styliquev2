## COMPANY VISION
Stylique Fashion is the **AI Sales Engine and Commerce Intelligence Layer for Fashion Brands.**
It sits on top of an ecommerce store and transforms every product page into an intelligent
selling experience while continuously learning from customer behavior to improve the entire
business. Stylique does not simply answer questions. It **sells. It learns. It recommends. It
analyzes. It improves.** Every customer interaction becomes an opportunity to generate revenue
and business intelligence.

## CORE MISSION
Help fashion brands: increase conversion rate · increase average order value · sell complete
looks · reduce purchase hesitation · improve merchandising · understand customer demand ·
discover revenue opportunities · continuously improve through AI. Every interaction should make
both the shopper and the merchant smarter.

## POSITIONING
**Do NOT position as:** Virtual Try-On software · AI chatbot · AI stylist · Recommendation
widget · Fashion assistant · Creative platform · AI content generator.
**Position as:** **AI Sales Engine for Fashion Brands.**
- **Headline:** Increase Revenue From Every Product Page.
- **Subheadline:** Mira transforms every product page into an intelligent shopping experience
  that guides shoppers, recommends complete looks, finds the right fit, and continuously learns
  how to grow your business.

## PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
Everything must answer one of two questions: (1) Does this help the shopper buy? (2) Does this
help the merchant make more money? If neither — **remove it.**

## MIRA
Mira is not a chatbot. Mira is an **AI Sales Associate** — the best in-store sales rep.
Responsibilities: understand shopper intent · answer questions · recommend products · discover
needs · build complete outfits · recommend sizes · explain fit · handle objections · reduce
hesitation · cross-sell · upsell · help complete purchases. Proactive without being intrusive.
Always prioritizes business outcomes.

## FIT INTELLIGENCE
Mira understands body profile, size charts, product fit, sizing differences, fit confidence.
Recommends the best size naturally during conversation. Objective: reduce uncertainty, increase
conversion.

## VIRTUAL TRY-ON
Remains part of Stylique. **It is NOT the product. It is one capability inside Mira.** ("Let's
preview it on a model closest to your body profile." / "Let's try it on.") Try-on supports
selling; never the hero feature.

## COMPLETE LOOK ENGINE
Don't sell one product — complete the outfit (dress → shoes → bag → jewelry → outerwear →
accessories). Goal: increase AOV naturally.

## COMMERCE INTELLIGENCE ENGINE
Every shopper interaction becomes structured business intelligence — what customers want, what
prevents purchases, what demand exists, what opportunities generate revenue. This becomes the
merchant's **source of truth.**

### Sub-engines
- **Customer Demand Intelligence** — most searched/requested products, categories, colors, fits,
  fabrics, styles, silhouettes, price ranges, occasions, collections, trends; seasonal + regional
  demand shifts. Identify demand before competitors.
- **Size & Fit Intelligence** — popular sizes, fit preferences, oversized vs tailored demand,
  regional sizing, products causing hesitation vs confidence, sizing abandonment, recommendation
  success rate.
- **Shopping Behavior Intelligence** — journeys, hesitation points, abandonment reasons, repeated
  questions, purchase triggers, successful recommendation paths, browsing + decision patterns.
- **Bundle Intelligence** — frequently bought/requested together, complete-outfit + accessory +
  cross-sell + upsell opportunities. Increase AOV.
- **Merchandising Intelligence** — weak PDPs, confusing descriptions, missing imagery, poor
  navigation, underperformers, high-interest/low-conversion products, category weaknesses.
- **Assortment Gap Engine** (strongest moat) — products customers repeatedly search for but can't
  find (e.g. oversized white linen shirt, emerald evening dress, petite blazer, plus-size formal
  gown). Estimate revenue opportunity per missing demand. Help brands buy/produce what customers
  already ask for.
- **Revenue Opportunity Engine** — estimate revenue lost to missing products, poor merchandising,
  weak bundling, fit uncertainty, navigation issues, unanswered questions. Prioritize by $ impact.
- **Customer Preference Engine** — living AGGREGATE customer profile: colors, styles, occasions,
  fabrics, price ranges, combinations, silhouettes, seasonal + regional preferences.
- **Execution Engine** — don't just identify, recommend actions (create a bundle, restock a size,
  introduce a missing color, improve imagery/descriptions, highlight a bestseller, reorganize
  collections, create a category). Future: merchant approves → Stylique executes automatically.

## SHOPIFY APP
The deployment mechanism — customers install via Shopify. Phase 1 is **demo-led**; the app
supports onboarding, it is NOT the marketing message.

## SALES STRATEGY (Phase 1, one flow)
Website → Book Demo → Meeting → Shopify URL collected → Automatic store analysis → Business Score
→ Cost Score → Recommended package → Personalized demo → Proposal → Payment → Shopify install →
Guided onboarding → Go live.
**No free trial. No direct purchase. No self-serve checkout.** Maximize close rate + collect
market feedback.

## BUSINESS SCORE ENGINE (internal only)
Inputs: annual online revenue, monthly orders, catalog size + complexity, merchandising maturity,
ecommerce maturity, expected AI workload, traffic estimate, support expectation, operational
complexity. Outputs: Business Score · Cost Score · Recommended Plan · Estimated Monthly Cost ·
Expected Margin · Upgrade Recommendation.

## INTERNAL PROFITABILITY ENGINE (hidden)
Track AI/infra/storage/db/monitoring/onboarding-amortization/support/engineering cost + gross
margin per merchant. If economics exceed the intended tier consistently for multiple months →
internal upgrade recommendation. Never react to temporary spikes.

dashboards, duplicate pages, vanity metrics, clutter, features without revenue impact. Every
screen answers: "How does this help the merchant sell more or make a better business decision?"

## LONG-TERM VISION
AI Sales Engine → Commerce Intelligence Layer understanding customers, products, merchandising,
assortment, demand, fit, trends, revenue opportunities → the operating intelligence layer for

## FINAL PHILOSOPHY
Every shopper interaction creates value twice: it helps the shopper buy the right product, and it
teaches the merchant how to build a better business. Mira gets smarter, the store gets smarter,
the merchandising + assortment get smarter, revenue grows. Stylique is not selling AI or virtual
try-on — it is building the intelligence layer that powers the future of fashion commerce.
