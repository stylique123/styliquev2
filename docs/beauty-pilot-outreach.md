## The 3 questions to open a conversation with

Use on LinkedIn DM or as the hook in discovery calls:

1. *"If I showed you a list of the 5 shades your shoppers asked for that you don't carry — what would you do with it?"*
2. *"What % of your foundation returns say 'wrong shade' in the reason?"*
3. *"Do you know which of your current customers are 2 weeks from running out of their last serum?"*

If they can't answer 2 or 3 of these, they need the product.

## Objections + rebuttals

**"We already have a quiz."**
> Most quizzes collect data and then send a generic email. Ours surfaces the result *at the moment of purchase*, adapts to every page the shopper visits, and tells you which shades you're missing at the brand level — the quiz doesn't do that.

**"Our team doesn't have time to manage an AI tool."**
> There's nothing to manage. The AI learns your catalog automatically and the dashboard is read-only. Set it up once; it runs itself.

**"We're worried about data privacy."**
> Shopper data never leaves Stylique's infrastructure. Brands see aggregated signals only — no individual transcripts, no personal data exports. We're GDPR/CCPA-compliant by default.

**"We're not sure our catalog is big enough."**
> ShadeMatch works with as few as 8 foundation shades. Routine Builder works with as few as 5 products across categories. Most clean beauty brands qualify immediately.

## Demo walk-through (15 min)

1. **(0–2 min)** Open the Lumière Skin demo store. Show a shopper landing on a PDP with the widget visible.
2. **(2–5 min)** Step 1 — Skin Profile: select skin type "combination", concerns "acne + dark spots". Show how the UI captures it without feeling clinical.
3. **(5–8 min)** Step 2 — Shade Match: show the foundation shades ranked by undertone match, confidence score per shade, "why this shade" tooltip.

5. **(11–13 min)** Merchant Dashboard: show the "Beauty Intelligence" tile — top concerns, shade gap count, replenishment signals.
6. **(13–15 min)** Q&A + pilot offer.
