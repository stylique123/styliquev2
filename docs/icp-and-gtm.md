# Stylique Beauty — ICP & GTM Playbook

*Last updated: 2026-05-30*

---

## Ideal Customer Profile

**Target:** DTC beauty brand, $500K–$10M ARR, Shopify, 2–10 person team, no dedicated analytics or AI headcount.

### Top Pain Points (ranked by frequency in DTC beauty)

1. Shade/undertone matching — shoppers abandon without "will this work on me?" confidence
2. Skin-type product fit — wrong recommendation = return + trust loss
3. Returns from mismatched expectations (shade, texture, scent)
4. Low repeat purchase rate — rely on ads instead of retention
5. Replenishment timing — no system to predict when a customer runs out
6. Rising CAC; LTV math only works if repeat purchase does
7. Small team can't staff real CX personalization
8. App stack bloat — 10–15 apps, $2K–$5K/mo, page load penalty
9. Klaviyo flows are generic — replenishment sequences aren't per-SKU or per-skin-type
10. Okendo/Yotpo collect skin data but don't feed it back into product recs
11. AOV stuck — cross-sell and bundling are manual or generic
12. No connection between quiz data and on-site behavior
13. Subscription churn — ReCharge/Skio doesn't explain pause reasons
14. Attribution chaos — Triple Whale tells you what spent, not what drove repeat
15. UGC doesn't connect to product discovery on-site
16. Fragrance/sensorial products can't be sampled — pre-purchase anxiety
17. Decision fatigue from large shade ranges
18. No "memory" of past purchases feeding new recs
19. Ingredient/formulation anxiety — shoppers leave to check Reddit/TikTok
20. Social proof is siloed — reviews don't surface at the right moment

### Current Stack Gaps

| Tool | What It Does | The Gap |
|---|---|---|
| **Klaviyo** | Email/SMS, replenishment flows | Generic segments; no real-time on-site personalization |
| **Gorgias** | CX ticketing | Reactive, not proactive; can't prevent the question |
| **Yotpo / Okendo** | Reviews + UGC | Collects skin attributes but doesn't loop back into recs |
| **ReCharge / Skio** | Subscriptions | No churn-reason insight; no "suggest the right cadence" |
| **Triple Whale / Northbeam** | Attribution | Ad spend, not LTV or per-SKU repeat-purchase rate |
| **Loop Returns** | Returns management | No pre-return intervention (shade swap, guidance) |

**Universal gap:** none of these tools talk to each other in real time at the point of decision.

### Decision-Maker Titles
1. **Founder / CEO** (primary) — owns P&L, approves any tool >$200/mo
2. **Head of Ecommerce / DTC** — evaluates and implements
3. **CMO / Head of Marketing** — cares about AOV, repeat rate, LTV impact

At $500K–$10M, often one person holds 2–3 of these titles.

### ROI Metrics to Own in Pitch
| Metric | Stylique Lever |
|---|---|
| **Return rate** | Pre-purchase shade/fit guidance = fewer "wrong product" returns |
| **Repeat purchase rate** | Mira remembers past purchases, surfaces replenishment at right time |
| **AOV** | Complete-the-look, smart cross-sell = 20–35% AOV lift |
| **Conversion rate** | Removes the "will this work on me?" abandon moment |
| **Subscription retention** | Proactive regimen guidance keeps subscribers engaged |

## 10 Sub-Niches to Target First

| # | Sub-niche | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clean/natural skincare** | Largest indie segment; ingredient anxiety is acute |
| 2 | **Indie fragrance** | Sales up 46% YoY 2025; highest pre-purchase anxiety; "scent matchmaker" wedge |
| 3 | **K-beauty (Western DTC)** | Complex multi-step routines = high guidance need; TikTok Shop drives Shopify traffic |
| 4 | **Shade-range cosmetics** | Shade matching is #1 abandonment driver; Mira's selfie→rec is direct |
| 5 | **Skincare subscriptions / regimens** | ReCharge/Skio adoption 2× beauty vs. overall; churn-prevention unserved |
| 6 | **Wellness-beauty crossover** | High ingredient anxiety; repeat purchase depends on "is it working?" |
| 7 | **Indie lip/color cosmetics** | Formula + shade complexity; complete-the-look AOV play |
| 8 | **Men's grooming DTC** | Fastest-growing sub-segment; low brand loyalty; non-judgmental advisor |
| 9 | **Postpartum / hormonal skincare** | Intense guidance need; underserved by generic tools |
| 10 | **Sustainable/refillable beauty** | Brand story complexity is chat-native; loyalty + replenishment cadence maps perfectly |

**Recommended first beachhead:** Clean skincare ($500K–$3M ARR, founder-led, Klaviyo + Okendo installed) — largest addressable pool, highest guidance anxiety, fastest path to a "return rate down + repeat rate up" case study.

### App Store ASO Keywords

### 3 Highest-Leverage Actions: First 30 Days

**Day 1–7: App Store listing hardened**
Rewrite listing around merchant outcomes. Add shade match, skin quiz, AI routine builder, replenishment to title/subtitle/keyword fields. Set up 3-email onboarding sequence with review ask at day 7.

**Day 8–20: Community seeding, value-first**
Post a genuinely useful resource (e.g. "5 skincare quiz flows that convert above 18%") in Beauty Independent's community, Indie Beauty Network Slack, and Femstreet groups. No pitch. Answer DMs. First 3–5 installs typically come from this.

**Day 21–30: One agency co-sell partnership locked**
Common Thread Collective or Needle — offer free white-glove setup for their next beauty client in exchange for a case study and referral agreement. One locked agency partner > 200 cold emails in this segment.

### Anchoring Rationale
- **$599 Ultimate** — below the "call sales" threshold ($1K+/mo); 2.1× routine AOV on a $5M brand = $2M influenced revenue, $7,188/yr = 0.36% take rate; Rebuy's top plan is $749/mo for generic recs — we charge less for a vertical-specific AI layer
- **$249 Growth** — hits "under $3K/year" pre-approved budget threshold at $1M–$5M brands
- **$79 Starter** — accessible entry; if routine builder moves one shopper/day from single-SKU to 3-piece, math pays out before month 2

### Price Objection Scripts

**"This is too expensive for us" ($5K ARR brand)**
> "At your stage, Starter at $79/mo is $948/year. If the routine builder moves even one shopper per day from a single-SKU buy to a 3-piece routine, the math pays out before month two. Start there — upgrade when the dashboard shows you the lift."

**"We already have Klaviyo/Rebuy" ($5M ARR brand)**
> "Klaviyo sends the email after the decision. Rebuy recommends what to add to the cart. Neither knows the shopper's skin — not their concern type, not their undertone, not whether they've been replenishing on time. Your return rate is real money. We close that gap before the decision, not after. And we give you intelligence data to brief your buyers on what the catalog is missing."
