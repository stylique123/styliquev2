# Stylique Beauty — Competitive Intelligence

*Last updated: 2026-05-30*

## The Big Four — What They Are and Why They Don't Serve Our Buyer

### Perfect Corp (YouCam)
- **Pricing:** B2B enterprise licensing (custom quotes); Shopify app ~$99–$299/mo for basic AR try-on; full suite is five-figure annual deals
- **Core features:** AR virtual makeup try-on, shade finder, skin analysis, AI Beauty Agent (2025), API
- **Notable clients:** L'Oréal, Tom Ford, Neutrogena, Ulta
- **Gaps:** No shade-gap analytics. No replenishment engine. No routine AOV optimization. Analytics locked to enterprise tier. Consumption-based pricing punishes growing brands.

### Revieve
- **Pricing:** 100% custom enterprise contracts. No self-serve. Minimum ~$2,000–$5,000+/mo. ~250 brand clients.
- **Core features:** AI skin analysis, foundation shade matching, haircare advisor, makeup try-on, GenAI discovery (2025), Shopify integration (implementation-heavy)
- **Notable clients:** JCPenney, Super-Pharm, AS Watson, BABOR
- **Gaps:** Zero self-serve path — a 5-person skincare brand cannot buy without a sales cycle. No replenishment. No shade-gap intelligence. Analytics = engagement metrics, not unmet demand.

### Haut.AI
- **Pricing:** Usage-based (not public); reported entry ~$500–$1,500/mo. Shopify + WooCommerce no-code widget available.
- **Core features:** 150 facial biomarker analysis, SkinGPT (routine visualization), Skin Atlas privacy tech, product recs, no-code widget, API. Claims 34% AOV lift.
- **Notable clients:** Beiersdorf, Ulta Beauty, Neutrogena, Grupo Boticário
- **Gaps:** Recommendations are one-shot, not lifecycle-aware. No shade-gap report. No replenishment engine. Analytics show what was recommended; don't surface unmet needs. Still requires onboarding support.

### ModiFace (L'Oréal)
- **Pricing:** L'Oréal-owned — unavailable to independent brands. API licensing targets enterprise/retail platform partners only.
- **Core features:** Makeup AR try-on, nail/hair AR, skin diagnostics, 100M+ sessions/yr
- **Notable clients:** L'Oréal brands exclusively, Sephora, Amazon as integration partners
- **Gaps:** Entirely inaccessible to a $500K–$10M brand. No analytics product. No replenishment. No shade-gap intelligence.

## The Three Gaps None of Them Cover

### Gap 1 — Shade Analytics (the unseen demand signal)
Every competitor tells shoppers what to buy. None tell brands what shades their shoppers searched for and couldn't find. Stylique Beauty turns every failed shade query into a catalog gap card with revenue at stake.

**Killer positioning:** *"Your competitors know what they sold. You need to know what you almost sold. Stylique Beauty's shade-gap dashboard turns every unmatched skin tone query into a named revenue opportunity — sorted by frequency, flagged for your next PO."*

### Gap 2 — Replenishment Engine (the retention money left on the table)
All four platforms are one-shot recommendation engines. None model product lifecycle, send re-engagement at the right cadence, or route through the brand's Klaviyo flow.

**Killer positioning:** *"Haut.AI ends at checkout. We don't. Stylique Beauty models every product's usage lifecycle, triggers re-engagement at the moment repurchase intent is highest, and plugs directly into Klaviyo — no dev work, no new subscription SKU required."*

### Gap 3 — Routine AOV Optimization (the $0 upsell nobody is making)
Neither platform surfaces to the brand: "Shoppers who complete a 3-step AM routine spend 2.4× more, yet 68% of your recommendation sessions end at a single product."

**Killer positioning:** *"A skincare routine is worth 2–4× a single product sale. Revieve shows consumers a routine. We show you which routines in your catalog are converting and which sessions are ending one product too early — then we fix it."*

### On accessibility (vs all competitors)
*"ModiFace requires an L'Oréal partnership. Revieve requires a sales cycle. Haut.AI requires an onboarding project. Stylique Beauty requires 10 minutes and a Shopify login. Same AI advisor, none of the enterprise tax."*
